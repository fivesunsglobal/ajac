Why AJAC Info Integrity Exists

In the last decade, AI-powered manipulation, aggressive geopolitical actors, a collapse in trust, and coordinated cross-platform operations have created a threat landscape that no existing solution fully addresses.

The existing toolkit - public relations, cybersecurity, and social media monitoring - was built for a different era.

At the same time, mis/disinformation is the World Economic Forum's #2 global short-term risk, above even economic shocks. Every business, government, and civic actor is exposed.

Who We Work With

AJAC helps high-exposure organizations build systems to mitigate narrative and information risks. We leverage deep expertise to bridge the gap between threat detection and strategic response. Our method is designed for organizations facing multi-faceted exposure to geopolitical, social, and synthetic narrative threats.

What we do

Assess

We identify your specific information manipulation or disinformation vulnerabilities and evaluate the effectiveness of your current response infrastructure.

Build

We build custom standardized workflows and training systems for your organization to maintain a permanent and repeatable internal and external response capability, tailored to your unique risk profile and industry.

Connect

We provide access to a specialized, global network of experts and frameworks to facilitate coordinated and informed action to reduce and mitigate risks that undermine your core mission.
